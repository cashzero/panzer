# M4A2(76)W / VVSS reference

The model follows the [D. P. Dyer four-view M4A2(76)W drawing](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/usa/us018m4a276wp.html), downloaded and inspected locally as `tmp/m4a2-76-reference.jpg`. This is the large-hatch, welded-hull, diesel M4A2 with T23 turret and narrow VVSS tracks, not a 75 mm Sherman or an HVSS Easy Eight.

Distinctive geometry:

- Approximately 47-degree-from-vertical upper glacis, flush large oval crew hatches, no early cast driver hoods.
- A single rounded T23 casting with a longer bustle and raised rear underside, wide M62 rotor shield, right vision cupola and left loader hatch.
- Long 76 mm M1 barrel with a thread-protector outline and open muzzle, matching the drawing rather than retaining the previous oversized brake.
- Twin diesel-engine deck grilles, rear exhaust louvres and folding rear shelf; no radial-engine access doors or external air cleaners copied from the M4.
- Three paired VVSS bogies per side and continuous narrow tracks, reusing the corrected M4 running-gear construction.

The roof MG is retained as a simplified equipment detail. Shapes and collision coverage are game-scale approximations. Existing combat values, armor thickness, mobility and traverse limits are preserved. Mounts, muzzle distance and armor box geometry are aligned with the changed model.

Local checks: `tmp/validate-m4a2-rework.ts`, `tmp/check-m4a2-rework.mjs`; screenshots cover orthographic front/side/top, perspective front/rear, gun movement and destroyed materials. Downloaded drawings remain local research artifacts.
