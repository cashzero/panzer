# Panther Ausf. A (Pz.Kpfw. V, Sd.Kfz. 171) visual reference

The model is a Panther Ausf. A of late 1943 to mid 1944: welded hull with the
55 degree glacis, hull MG 34 in a ball mount, driver's visor, Schurzen on a rail
along the sponsons, interleaved 860 mm road wheels on eight stations, the
Ausf. A turret with the cast commander's cupola and AA ring, smoke dischargers
on the turret sides, the rounded cast mantlet and the 7.5 cm KwK 42 L/70 with its
double-baffle muzzle brake. It follows the H.L. Doyle four-view drawing
published by [OnWar](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/germany/ge023panthera.html)
([drawing image](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/germany/ge023panthera.jpg))
and is a game-scale approximation, not a dimensionally certified replica. It is
not a Panther D (no cupola, pistol ports) or G (straight-sided hull, no visor).

`model.json` and `tank.json` are generated by
[`docs/references/panther_a/generate.mjs`](../../../docs/references/panther_a/generate.mjs);
edit its parameters and regenerate rather than hand-editing the JSON. Sources,
the orthographic calibration passes, residuals and limitations are recorded in
[the calibration record](../../../docs/references/panther_a/README.md).

Coordinates are meters, +Z forward and +Y up. "left" in node and plate ids is
-X, matching the `tracksLeft` slot and `isTrack: "left"` used by the other
tanks; the vehicle's own left (driver, cupola, antenna) is +X. The hull is one
polyhedron: a cross-section with sponsons, cut by the upper and lower glacis
planes at the front and the undercut rear plate at the back. The track outline
runs under the wheels, round the sprocket and idler, and over the wheel tops
(the upper run rests on them, as on the real vehicle); cleats and guide horns are
batched into one polyhedron per side. The turret is lofted between a base ring
and a roof ring.

Key dimensions: 6.72 m from the fender tips to the rear plate top (published
hull length 6.87 m), muzzle at Z=5.335 m and rear plate top at Z=−3.219 m
(8.55 m; OnWar gives 8.66 m), 3.30 m over the tracks (published 3.27 m), 3.45 m
over the Schurzen (3.42 m), 660 mm tracks on 2.64 m centres, 860 mm road wheels,
1.906 m hull roof, 2.668 m turret roof, 2.31 m gun axis.

## Gameplay data

- Armour follows the OnWar specification for the Ausf. A: upper glacis 80 mm and
  lower glacis 60 mm at 55 degrees, superstructure sides 40 mm at 40 degrees,
  lower hull sides 40 mm, rear 40 mm at 30 degrees, hull roof and engine deck
  16 mm; turret front 100 mm, sides and rear 45 mm at 25 degrees, roof 16 mm,
  cast mantlet 100 mm, cupola 80 mm. The Schurzen are 5 mm plates, as on the
  Panzer IV Ausf. H. The catalog summary reports the glacis (80), sides (40),
  rear (40) and turret front (100); combat physics applies impact angles separately.
- 7.5 cm KwK 42 L/70: Pzgr. 39/42 (AP) 925 m/s, 138/124/111/99/89 mm at
  100/500/1000/1500/2000 m against 30 degree plate; Pzgr. 40/42 (APC slot,
  tungsten core) 1120 m/s, 194/174/149/127 mm; Sprgr. 42 (HE) 700 m/s.
  Elevation −8° to +18°.
- Hydraulic traverse driven from the engine: 0.27 rad/s is the high-speed ratio
  at 2000 rpm (about 23 s per revolution; 15 s at 3000 rpm, 46 s at 1000 rpm),
  a mid-rpm figure like the Tiger I's.
- 700 hp / 44.8 t; acceleration uses the Tiger I's acceleration per hp/t. Health
  310, reload 6 s, 12 m/s top speed and the damage values are game-balance
  values set between the Panzer IV and the Tiger I.
