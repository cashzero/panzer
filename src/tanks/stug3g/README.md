# StuG III Ausf. G (7.5 cm Sturmgeschütz 40 Ausf. G, Sd.Kfz. 142/1) visual reference

The model is a late StuG III Ausf. G (from late 1943): Pz.Kpfw. III chassis with
six pairs of rubber-tyred road wheels, three return rollers, front sprocket and
rear idler; 50 + 30 mm nose and casemate front; the widened casemate with
sloped-top panniers; the cast Saukopf mantlet and 7.5 cm StuK 40 L/48 with its
double-baffle muzzle brake; commander's cupola; loader's MG 34 behind a shield;
Schurzen; spare road wheels and a stowage rack on the engine deck. It follows the
H.L. Doyle drawing published by
[OnWar](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/germany/ge064stug3g.html)
([drawing image](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/germany/ge064stug3g.jpg))
and is a game-scale approximation, not a dimensionally certified replica. It is
not an early G (box mantlet) or an Ausf. F/8.

`model.json` and `tank.json` are generated by
[`docs/references/stug3g/generate.mjs`](../../../docs/references/stug3g/generate.mjs);
edit its parameters and regenerate rather than hand-editing the JSON. Sources,
the orthographic calibration passes, residuals and limitations are recorded in
[the calibration record](../../../docs/references/stug3g/README.md).

## Turretless layout

The casemate belongs to the hull. The `turret` slot holds only the gun's
traversing cradle, hidden inside the casemate; `mounts.turretOffset` is its
pivot, under the trunnions, and the Saukopf, barrel and brake sit in the `gun`
slot so they traverse and elevate with the gun. No armour plate has `turret` as
its parent. `traverse.limitDeg: 10` limits the gun to 10 degrees each side of
the centreline: player aiming and both AIs clamp to it (`src/traverseLimit.ts`),
and AI crews turn the hull to bring the gun to bear.

Coordinates are meters, +Z forward and +Y up. "left" in node and plate ids is
-X, matching the `tracksLeft` slot and `isTrack: "left"` used by the other
tanks; the vehicle's own left (driver, commander's cupola) is +X. The gun is
offset 0.113 m to the vehicle's right.

Key dimensions: muzzle at Z=4.039 m, hull rear at Z=−2.63 m, 2.94 m over the
tracks, 400 mm tracks, 3.33 m over the splayed Schurzen, 514 mm road wheels,
1.355 m fender line, 2.04 m casemate roof, 1.592 m gun axis, 2.23 m cupola.

## Gameplay data

- Armour follows the OnWar specification: Saukopf 80 mm, casemate front 50 + 30
  mm, sloped upper front 50 mm, nose 50 + 30 mm over a 50 mm lower nose,
  panniers and sides 30 mm, engine sides 30 mm, hull rear 50 mm, upper rear and
  casemate rear 30 mm, roof 17 mm, engine deck 16 mm, cupola 30 mm. The Schurzen
  are 5 mm plates, as on the Panzer IV Ausf. H and Panther. The catalog summary
  reports front 80, side 30, rear 50 and the Saukopf (80) in the turret slot,
  which the tank select plate labels "mantlet" for turretless vehicles.
- 7.5 cm StuK 40 L/48: the same gun as the Panzer IV Ausf. H's KwK 40, so the
  AP, APC and HE values are the Panzer IV's. Elevation −6° to +20°, hand
  traverse 10° each side at 0.05 rad/s (the 20° arc in about 7 s).
- 300 hp / 23.9 t; acceleration uses the Panzer III's acceleration per hp/t.
  Health 230, reload 5 s and 10 m/s top speed are game-balance values.
