# M10 GMC (3-inch Gun Motor Carriage M10) visual reference

The model is the late-production M10 on the diesel M4A2 chassis: VVSS running
gear, welded upper hull with 38 degree sloped sides, open-topped pentagonal
turret with the June 1943 "duckbill" counterweight, 3-inch Gun M7 in Mount M5,
and a .50 cal M2 stowed facing aft on the rear pintle. It follows the
[OnWar four-view drawing](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/usa/us021m10p.html)
([drawing image](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/usa/us021m10.jpg)) and is a
game-scale approximation, not a dimensionally certified replica. It is not an
M10A1 (M4A3 chassis) or an early wedge-counterweight vehicle.

`model.json` and `tank.json` are generated by
[`docs/references/m10/generate.mjs`](../../../docs/references/m10/generate.mjs);
edit its parameters and regenerate rather than hand-editing the JSON. Sources,
the orthographic calibration passes, residuals and limitations are recorded in
[the calibration record](../../../docs/references/m10/README.md).

Coordinates are meters, +Z forward and +Y up. "left" in node and plate ids is
-X, matching the `tracksLeft` slot and `isTrack: "left"` used by the other
tanks. Track outlines use local XY (-world Z, world Y) extruded along the axle;
the belt is routed on tangents around the wheels, sprocket and idler, and the
cleats plus the guide horns visible under the upper run are batched into one
polyhedron per side. The turret shell is a single polyhedron lofted through
bottom, knee and top rings, hollow for the open fighting compartment and solid
through the counterweight; the turret race top is the visible floor.

Key dimensions: 5.96 m hull with fenders, 6.83 m with gun forward, 3.05 m wide,
2.529 m across the tracks (83 in tread, 421 mm track), 508 mm road wheels,
1.793 m hull roof, 2.57 m turret top, 2.192 m gun axis, muzzle at Z=3.89 m.

## Gameplay data

- Armour follows the [OnWar M10 specification](https://www.onwar.com/wwii/tanks/usa/us021m10.html):
  differential housing 51 mm, glacis 38 mm, sloped sides/rear 19 mm, lower hull
  rear 25 mm, roof 13 mm, turret walls and counterweight 25 mm, gun shield 57 mm.
  The catalog summary reports the glacis (38), upper side (19), rear (19) and
  gun shield (57). Combat physics applies impact angles separately.
- The turret has **no roof plate**: plunging fire that enters the open top hits
  the walls or the 13 mm hull roof below. `armor.test.ts` checks this together
  with frontal/side/rear/track coverage and hitbox-to-surface distances.
- 3-inch M7: the M79 AP and M62 APC use the same projectiles and 792 m/s as the
  76 mm M1, so their damage values and 30 degree penetration points match the
  M4A2(76); HE M42A1 is 853 m/s. Elevation −10° to +30° (OnWar). Hand traverse
  0.078 rad/s (about 80 s per revolution, as documented for the M10).
- 375 hp / 29.6 t; acceleration uses the Sherman's hp/t ratio. Health, reload
  and speed are game-balance values chosen alongside the existing tanks.

Run `node --import tsx --test --test-isolation=none src/tanks/m10/armor.test.ts`
after changing plates or geometry.
